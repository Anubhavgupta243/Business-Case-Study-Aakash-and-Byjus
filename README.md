📉→📈 Aakash-Byju's Strategic Revival & Debt Restructuring

Biz Rescue Competition | NIT Raipur E-Summit Anantya 2026 | Finalist
Team: Startaland — Strategy + Grounded Execution


🧩 Problem Statement
Byju's, once India's most valued EdTech unicorn (~$22B), collapsed under mounting debt, operational chaos, and a trust deficit following its acquisition of Aakash Educational Services. The challenge: design a credible, actionable revival roadmap — covering debt restructuring, operational efficiency, and brand restoration — for a company most had already written off.

🔍 Our Approach
Rather than avoiding the hardest problem statement at the competition, Team Startaland chose it deliberately. Having experienced the Aakash-Byju's ecosystem firsthand as a student — both pre and post-acquisition — the analysis was grounded in real operational insight, not just theory.

Key Pillars of the Strategy

Debt Restructuring - Phased repayment model, asset monetisation, and creditor negotiation framework
Operational Efficiency - Cost rationalisation across redundant verticals post-merger
Revenue Diversification - Re-focus on Aakash's profitable offline coaching core; B2B institutional licensing
Trust Restoration - Transparency-first communication strategy for students, parents & investors
Brand SeparationDecouple Aakash brand equity from Byju's reputational damage
